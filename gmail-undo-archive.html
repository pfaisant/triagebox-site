How undo archive actually runs

  1. Install and connect Gmail. Desktop Chrome. No TriageBox account.
  2. Archive or delete from the popup. A toast offers Undo. The removal is also stored in recentRemovals (local storage).
  3. Open Recently removed. ↩ beside Refresh. Last 24 hours, newest 20. Each row: subject, from, Archived or Deleted, Restore.
  4. Restore. Back to the inbox (and unread when that was recorded). Trash items restore from the trash path. On another mailbox the button says Switch first. Bulk-sweep undos appear when sweep-undo-list is available.

Quick answers

Undo archive Gmail

How do I undo archive in Gmail from a Chrome extension?

After you archive or delete from the TriageBox popup, a toast offers Undo. There is also a persistent Recently removed tray (↩ beside Refresh). It keeps the last 24 hours, newest 20 rows, with subject, from, Archived vs Deleted, and a Restore button. Restores go back to the inbox (and unread when that was recorded). Trash items use the trash restore path. No AI key. No TriageBox server. Desktop Chrome only.

What does Switch first mean on Restore?

Restore uses the active mailbox token. If the row was removed under another connected account, the button is locked as Switch first until you switch to that mailbox.

Is this the same as keep unread, spam review, or delete old mail?

No. Keep unread leaves a conversation unread after peeking. Spam review Delete entire folder empties Spam. Delete old mail is an age sweep. This page is undo/restore for archive and delete from the popup, including recent bulk-sweep undos when available.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in your browser; Gmail data is not sent to the publisher.
