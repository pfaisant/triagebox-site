Private by design

Your inbox does not pass through a TriageBox server

The purpose of TriageBox AI Gmail Checker is to help the signed-in user manage and prioritise their own Gmail inbox from the browser. The app is a productivity Chrome extension (toolbar badge + popup / side panel) that, with the user’s OAuth consent, accesses the user’s Gmail and optionally Google Calendar so they can: view unread mail; archive, snooze, label, trash, or mark threads read/unread; review possible phishing risk; see their calendar agenda; and optionally generate AI summaries using API keys the user supplies.

TriageBox AI Gmail Checker has no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in the user’s browser; data is not sent to the publisher. This homepage is the official product site for the application of the same name configured on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Quick answers

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Free to install on the Chrome Web Store — no subscription and no TriageBox account. Optional support never unlocks features.

Does mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in your browser; your Gmail data is not sent to the publisher.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Triage, sweeps, unsubscribe and the no-key scam checks work without one. Summaries and AI scores are optional if you add a Gemini and/or xAI key.

Will it replace Checker Plus for Gmail?

Not as a notifier — Checker Plus remains stronger there. TriageBox is built to clear the pile. Honest comparison.

Who it is for

Individuals who want a focused inbox workbench for their own Google account. It is not a bulk-mail tool, not a marketing platform, and not intended to access other people’s mailboxes.

How sign-in works

Google sign-in uses OAuth via Chrome’s identity APIs. You grant access on Google’s consent screen. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the extension removes local data stored by the app.
