How deleting old Gmail actually runs

Add to Chrome installs this product, not the in-extension nightly log. These are the steps on a signed-in Gmail account you are authorised to use.

  1. Install and connect Gmail. Desktop Chrome or another Chromium browser. Google OAuth is still unverified, so the first run shows Google’s warning screen. No TriageBox account.
  2. Open Bulk sweep. Quick groups need no scan: Older than 1 year, 6 months or 3 months; Promotions / Social / Updates / Forums; already-read; larger than 5 MB. Each row fetches its exact count on demand.
  3. Confirm the live number. Archive, file or delete a group only after a recount. Starred and Gmail-important can be excluded. Delete is red, behind a second confirmation; past 200 threads you type DELETE. You can narrow any group with an age refine (48 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year) or unread / already-read — the confirm dialog names the filter.
  4. Undo if you were wrong. Recently removed is a restore tray. Archived mail stays in All Mail. Deleted mail sits in Gmail’s Trash for 30 days. A sender scan (for grouping by domain) reads up to 20,000 messages by default and can resume after a stop.

Quick answers

Delete old emails in Gmail

How do I delete old emails in Gmail?

Gmail on the web can already do this: search older_than:1y (or older_than:6m), tick the page, click “Select all conversations that match this search”, then Trash. TriageBox 1.26.2 is a Chrome extension that puts the same age bands on a Bulk sweep page — Older than 1 year, 6 months or 3 months — fetches an exact count without a metadata scan, and shows “412 to archive, 23 starred kept” before anything moves. Archive is the default; delete is red, behind a second confirmation; past 200 threads you type DELETE.

Does TriageBox delete everything older than a year in one click?

No. There is no wipe-the-mailbox button. You pick a group (an age band, Promotions, a sender, a domain, or mail larger than 5 MB), see a live recount, then confirm. You can narrow any group further with older_than (48 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year) and unread/already-read. Starred and Gmail-important mail can be excluded.

Is this the same as bulk unsubscribe?

No. Unsubscribe asks a sender to stop future mail. Deleting old emails clears mail that is already in the mailbox. They are different jobs and different pages. Use bulk unsubscribe Gmail to stop a list per exact sender; use this page when the problem is age, Promotions, or storage.

Do I need an AI key to delete or archive old Gmail?

No. Badge, popup actions, bulk sweeps, unsubscribe and the no-key scam checks work with nothing configured. Summaries and 0–100 scores are optional if you add a Gemini and/or xAI key. A key is not a subscription.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in your browser; Gmail data is not sent to the publisher. Optional AI requests go from your browser to the provider whose key you added.
