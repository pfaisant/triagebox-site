How mute sender actually runs

  1. Install and connect Gmail. Desktop Chrome. No TriageBox account.
  2. Open the card more menu. Choose Mute sender. TriageBox stores that address in unimportantSenders and filters it out of Unread/Read (markUnimportant).
  3. Open the Muted tab. It appears when it has content. Muted mail collects there — nothing was deleted.
  4. Unmute when you want them back. From the Muted tab, Unmute sender (markImportant), or remove the address under Settings → Muted senders. A toast Undo is offered right after mute.

Quick answers

Mute sender Gmail

How do I mute a sender in Gmail from a Chrome extension?

In TriageBox 1.72, open the card more menu and choose Mute sender. That email address is filtered out of Unread and Read and collected in the Muted tab. Unmute sender from the Muted tab restores the sender. The Muted tab appears when it has content. Mail is not deleted. This does not unsubscribe and does not empty Promotions. No AI key. No TriageBox server. Desktop Chrome only.

Does mute delete mail or unsubscribe the sender?

No. Mute only filters that sender out of Unread/Read into the Muted tab. Messages stay in the mailbox. Unsubscribe is a different card action (and bulk unsubscribe is a different page). Emptying Promotions is Bulk sweep on stop promotional emails.

Is this the same as stop promotions, bulk unsubscribe, or undo archive?

No. Stop promotions is category/age bulk sweep plus Normal mail only. Bulk unsubscribe is per exact sender then leftover sweep. Undo archive is toast Undo and the Recently removed tray. This page is mute/unmute one sender into the Muted tab.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in your browser; Gmail data is not sent to the publisher.
