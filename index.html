TriageBox AI Gmail Checker

A Chrome extension that helps you triage your own Gmail inbox — faster actions, smarter prioritisation, optional AI summaries. Your mail stays in your browser.

Purpose of this application. TriageBox AI Gmail Checker (also shown as “TriageBox” in the browser toolbar) is a productivity extension for Google Chrome and compatible Chromium browsers. It connects to your Gmail and Google Calendar accounts, with your consent, so you can see unread mail, act on threads (archive, snooze, label, trash, mark read), spot possible phishing, view your agenda, and optionally generate AI summaries using your own API keys. The extension has no developer backend: tokens and data stay on your device; API calls go directly from your browser to Google (and optional AI providers you configure).

What the app does

Gmail triage

Unread badge, thread list, archive, snooze, labels, mark read/unread, spam, trash — on your account only.

Safety signals

Optional risk / phishing scoring on messages you choose to analyse, to help you spot scams faster.

Calendar

Optional agenda view and event actions using Google Calendar scopes you approve.

Optional AI

Summaries and scores only when you supply a Gemini and/or xAI key and trigger a feature.

Who it is for

Individuals who want a focused inbox workbench for their own Google account. It is not a bulk-mail tool, not a marketing platform, and not intended to access other people’s mailboxes.

How sign-in works

Google sign-in uses OAuth via Chrome’s identity APIs. You grant access on Google’s consent screen. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the extension removes local data stored by the app.
