Quick answers

Switching from Checker Plus

Will TriageBox replace Checker Plus for Gmail?

Yes, for everyday popup mail actions and desktop alerts. TriageBox adds sender and domain cleanup, unsubscribe, spam review, calendar tools and optional AI triage. Checker Plus retains richer voice and notification controls, so compare those before switching.

Can I run Checker Plus and TriageBox together?

Yes. Both connect to Gmail from your browser. If you keep both during a trial, turn off arrival notifications in one to avoid duplicate alerts. Changes made by either tool appear in the same Gmail mailbox.

Do I need an AI key to use TriageBox as a Checker Plus alternative?

No. Connect Gmail for the badge, popup actions, bulk sweeps, unsubscribe and header-based scam checks. Summaries and AI ratings are optional and use your own Gemini, Grok or OpenRouter key. Provider limits and charges apply.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

There is no TriageBox mailbox backend. Mailbox work runs in your browser, and optional AI sends selected message content directly to your configured provider. The separate public support service counts anonymous likes without receiving Gmail content.

Is TriageBox free, like Checker Plus?

TriageBox core features are free, with no subscription or features unlocked by a donation. Optional AI-provider usage may cost money. On our reading of the Checker Plus contribution FAQ, its core notifier is free and contributions unlock listed extras.
