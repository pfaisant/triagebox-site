How the Waiting list actually runs

Add to Chrome installs this product, not the in-extension nightly log. These are the steps on a signed-in Gmail account you are authorised to use.

  1. Install and connect Gmail. Desktop Chrome or another Chromium browser. Google OAuth is still unverified, so the first run shows Google’s warning screen. No TriageBox account.
  2. Open the Waiting tab. Unread · Read · Archived · Sent are always visible; Waiting appears with the other extra tabs when it has content. The scan is in:sent -in:chats newer_than:14d, capped at 30 threads.
  3. Read who owes you. A thread stays only if the last message is one you sent (not a draft) and it has been unanswered for two days or more. The card shows days waiting. The “from” slot is who the last mail was To — the person who owes the reply.
  4. Nudge, or leave it. Write a threaded reply from the card, or open the thread in Gmail. Optional AI can draft the nudge if you added a Gemini or xAI key. TriageBox does not send unless you do. A 30-minute cache avoids re-scanning on every open.

Limits, stated

What this page does not claim

  • It does not list every unanswered email in the mailbox. Last 14 days, up to 30 sent threads, 2+ days with no reply, last message yours.
  • It does not replace Gmail Nudges. Those already work, for free, with no extension.
  • It does not show inbound mail you still owe a reply to. That is Gmail’s other Nudge, or your inbox.
  • It does not send follow-ups, schedule them, or cancel them if someone replies later.
  • It does not track opens. There is no pixel and no “they read it”.
  • It does not unsubscribe senders. That is bulk unsubscribe Gmail.
  • It does not delete old mail. That is delete old emails in Gmail.
  • It does not check phishing. That is Gmail phishing checker.
  • It does not run on your phone. Gmail’s own Nudges are what you have there.
  • It does not send mail through a TriageBox server. Optional AI goes from your browser to the key you added.
  • It is not for anyone else’s mailbox.

Quick answers

Gmail waiting for reply

How do I see emails waiting for a reply in Gmail?

Gmail’s Nudges (Settings → General → Nudges → Suggest emails to follow up on) can float a sent message back to the top of the inbox after a few days. Gmail decides which threads and when; there is no list of everything outstanding. TriageBox 1.26.2 is a Chrome extension with a Waiting tab: it scans sent threads from the last 14 days (up to 30), keeps those where the last message is yours and nobody has answered for 2+ days, and sorts them by days waiting. You can write a threaded nudge from the card. This is not open-tracking and it does not send follow-ups for you.

Does TriageBox list every unanswered email?

No. The scan is in:sent, not chats, newer than 14 days, at most 30 threads. A thread only appears if the last message is one you sent (not a draft) and it has been unanswered for two days or more. Older loops, inbound mail you still owe a reply to, and anything beyond that 30-thread window are out of scope. Results are cached for 30 minutes.

Is this Gmail Nudges, bulk unsubscribe, or deleting old emails?

No. Nudges is Gmail’s own heuristic bump inside the inbox. Unsubscribe stops future mail from a sender. Deleting old emails clears mail already in the mailbox by age or category. Phishing checks read headers on the card you are looking at. Waiting is only sent threads that still owe you a reply. Different jobs, different pages. Use bulk unsubscribe Gmail, delete old emails in Gmail, or Gmail phishing checker for those.

Do I need an AI key to see waiting-for-reply threads?

No. The Waiting list, the days-waiting badge, and writing a threaded nudge from the card work with nothing configured. Optional AI (your Gemini and/or xAI key) can draft the nudge in a chosen tone. A key is not a subscription. TriageBox never sends a follow-up unless you press send.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in your browser; Gmail data is not sent to the publisher. Optional AI requests go from your browser to the provider whose key you added. There is no tracking pixel and no open receipt.
