4a. Bulk actions, and what they mean

TriageBox can act on thousands of messages in one confirmed step — archive, apply a label, mark read, unsubscribe, or move to Trash — through its Bulk sweep and triage screens. Before any of that runs, the extension resolves the exact live count and shows it to you; large deletions ask for a second confirmation and, past a threshold, for a typed word. You confirm the action, and it is your mailbox: please read the count and the group it names before you press the button.

  • Archived and labelled mail stays in your account; Google keeps deleted mail in Trash for about 30 days and then removes it permanently. Recovery after that is a matter for Google, not for the publisher.
  • Unsubscribing sends a request to the sender or their unsubscribe service. Whether they honour it is outside the publisher's control, and it cannot be undone from here.
  • Counts come from Gmail at the moment of the action; a mailbox that changes while a sweep is running can produce a different final figure, and the extension reports what Google confirmed rather than what was requested.
  • The publisher provides no backup service. If mail matters to you, keep your own copy.

4b. AI output
