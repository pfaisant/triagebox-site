How bulk unsubscribe and sweep actually run

Add to Chrome installs this product, not the in-extension nightly log. These are the steps on a signed-in Gmail account you are authorised to use.

  1. Install and connect Gmail. Desktop Chrome or another Chromium browser. Google OAuth is still unverified, so the first run shows Google's warning screen. No TriageBox account.
  2. Unsubscribe per exact sender. From the popup or from spam review, send the List-Unsubscribe / one-click POST for that sender. Marketing can stop; statements from a different exact address at the same company are not included. Success is not something TriageBox can prove.
  3. Sweep the leftover pile with the number in front of you. One scan groups mail by sender, domain, category or age (cap 20,000 per scan; a stopped scan resumes). Archive or delete a group only after a live recount. Starred and Gmail-important mail can be excluded. Delete is red, behind a second confirmation; past 200 threads you type the word.
  4. Undo if you were wrong. Recently removed is a restore tray. Archived mail stays in All Mail. Deleted mail sits in Gmail's Trash for 30 days. Counts are what Gmail confirmed it changed, never what was requested.

Quick answers

Bulk unsubscribe Gmail

Can I bulk unsubscribe from Gmail?

Gmail's own Manage subscriptions view (rolling out on web and mobile) unsubscribes one sender at a time. TriageBox 1.26.2 is a Chrome extension that unsubscribes per exact sender — List-Unsubscribe / one-click POST, including from spam review — then lets you sweep that sender's leftover mail with a live count before anything moves. Whether the sender honours the request is outside TriageBox; success is not machine-verifiable.

Does TriageBox unsubscribe from every list at once?

No. Unsubscribe is per exact sender, so a company's statements can survive while its marketing stops. There is no "unsubscribe all 400 lists" button. You pick the sender, unsubscribe, then confirm an exact count to archive or delete the leftover pile.

What happens to old emails after I unsubscribe?

Unsubscribe only asks the sender to stop future mail. The backlog stays until you act. Bulk sweep groups by sender, domain, category or age, re-counts live ("412 to archive, 23 starred kept"), and archives, files or deletes that group after you confirm. Delete is red, behind a second confirmation; past 200 threads you type the word. Undo records what ran. Archived mail stays in All Mail; deleted mail sits in Gmail's Trash for 30 days.

Do I need an AI key to unsubscribe or sweep Gmail?

No. Badge, popup actions, bulk sweeps, unsubscribe and the no-key scam checks work with nothing configured. Summaries and 0–100 scores are optional if you add a Gemini and/or xAI key. A key is not a subscription.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. Processing runs in your browser; Gmail data is not sent to the publisher. Optional AI requests go from your browser to the provider whose key you added.
