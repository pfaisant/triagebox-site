How Summarize PDF actually runs

  1. Install and connect Gmail. Desktop Chrome. Add a Gemini, xAI, or OpenRouter key in Options. No TriageBox account.
  2. Open the card that has the PDF. The gold Summarize PDF button sits on the attachment preview. The menu says “Summarize PDF · name”.
  3. It reads the file. The button says Reading… while the model works. The summary appears above the viewer and scrolls into view. Over 4 MB is refused.
  4. Read it, or open the real PDF. Show summary brings the cached text back. Regenerate reads the attachment again. Nothing here files the document for you.

Quick answers

Gmail PDF summary

How do I summarize a PDF attached to a Gmail message?

TriageBox 1.57 is a Chrome extension. Open the attachment preview on the card, or the menu, and choose Summarize PDF. It reads that attached document and puts a summary on the card — dates, amounts, what it asks of you — without you opening the file in another app. This needs an AI key you add yourself (Gemini, xAI, or OpenRouter). It is not a no-key feature.

Does the summary read the PDF, or only the email subject?

The attachment. TriageBox sends that PDF’s bytes from your browser to the AI provider whose key you configured. A file over 4 MB is refused, the same ceiling as an email summary. The first two PDFs on a card get a Summarize PDF entry. Pictures are not offered this button. A scan that is only a picture of a page can come back thin.

Is this the same as summarizing the email thread?

No. Thread summary is the email. This page is the attached PDF. They can both exist on the same card. Neither replaces opening the real document if you need the pages themselves. The model can be wrong. A cached summary opens again without another read; Regenerate reads the file again. Sign-in codes are a different page.

Does my mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no developer-operated backend. The PDF goes from your browser to the provider whose key you added. Gmail data is not sent to the publisher.
