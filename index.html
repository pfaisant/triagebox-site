Privacy first

Your inbox does not pass through a TriageBox server

There is no TriageBox mail backend to trust. Mail moves between your browser and Google's APIs, and optional AI goes straight to the provider you chose.

No mailbox server

Processing runs in your browser. Your Gmail data is not sent to the publisher. The only shared service counts anonymous likes.

Keys stay on your device

AI keys live in this browser profile only, never in Chrome Sync, and are sent only to the provider that issued them.

Permissions you grant

Google OAuth through Chrome's identity APIs. Revoke any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; uninstalling removes local data.

Exact counts before bulk actions

Nothing is archived or deleted in bulk until you have seen the live number and confirmed. Trash keeps deleted mail for about 30 days.

What this app does, in one paragraph. The purpose of TriageBox AI Gmail Checker is to help the signed-in user manage and prioritise their own Gmail inbox from the browser. It is a productivity Chrome extension (toolbar badge, popup and side panel) that, with the user's OAuth consent, accesses the user's Gmail and optionally Google Calendar so they can view unread mail; archive, snooze, label, trash or mark threads read and unread; review possible phishing risk; see their calendar agenda; and optionally generate AI summaries using API keys the user supplies. It has no mailbox backend. This homepage is the official product site for the application of the same name configured on the Google OAuth consent screen. Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. Free to install on the Chrome Web Store, with no subscription and no TriageBox account. Optional support never unlocks features. Digital download only: nothing is shipped, and there is nothing to return or refund.

Does mail go through a TriageBox server?

No. There is no TriageBox mailbox backend. The separate public support service counts anonymous likes; it receives no Gmail content. Processing runs in your browser; your Gmail data is not sent to the publisher.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Triage, sweeps, unsubscribe and the no-key scam checks work without one. For summaries and scores there are two lanes: a free Gemini key keeps everything free, on Google's free allowance, and is enough for most inboxes; an OpenRouter key on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is what we recommend for comfort, with no daily quota and a cost of a fraction of a cent per summary. The welcome page walks you through both.

Will it replace Gmail?

No. TriageBox is not an email client. It works alongside Gmail from a popup or side panel; every action is a normal Gmail action on your own account, and Gmail stays exactly as it was.

Will it replace Checker Plus for Gmail?

Yes, for everyday popup mail actions and desktop alerts. TriageBox adds sender cleanup, unsubscribe, spam review and optional AI. Checker Plus retains richer voice and notification controls. Honest comparison, losses included.

Which browsers and phones?

Any Chromium browser that installs Chrome Web Store extensions: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Comet. On Android there is a native app, not yet on Google Play: request the APK and it arrives by email with its checksum. There is no iOS app.

Who is it for?

Individuals who want a focused inbox workbench for their own Google account. It is not a bulk-mail tool, not a marketing platform, and not intended to access other people's mailboxes.
